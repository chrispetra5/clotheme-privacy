What we collect

  • Usage information such as pages viewed, features used, timestamps, and error reports.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser, device and language, and the page that referred you.
  • Your email address only if you send it to us, for example when you contact us.

We do not knowingly process sensitive personal information.

The text you type into the extension

When you ask for an outfit, the text you type is sent to our servers and to our AI and search providers so we can build the outfit.

Services we use

  • Amazon Associates for affiliate links.
  • AI service providers to build the outfits.
  • Product search providers to find items on Amazon.
